# NOVUM — LORE REFERENCE

Version: 0.1  
Status: Active development reference

## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document is the canonical reference for NOVUM setting lore.

It records:

- confirmed setting facts
- current design principles
- provisional concepts
- unresolved questions
- explicitly rejected or retired ideas

Lore should not be treated as canon unless marked CONFIRMED.

Status tags:

- CONFIRMED — established setting canon
- PROVISIONAL — current working direction
- OPEN — unresolved
- RETIRED — no longer part of the setting

---

# 2. Core Setting Premise

## CONFIRMED

NOVUM is a science-fiction setting combining:

- cyberpunk social and economic structures
- dense urban environments
- megacorporations
- dynastic Houses
- interplanetary and interstellar civilisation
- hard-SF reasoning wherever practical

Scientific plausibility is the default, but selected speculative technologies may be used where they materially improve story or gameplay.

The setting should avoid becoming science fantasy simply for convenience.

The intended social tone combines:

- dense corporate and urban cyberpunk
- aristocratic and dynastic politics
- large-scale space-opera institutions
- grounded scientific and technological consequences

The game is expected to focus primarily on one star system, while retaining the possibility of travel to other systems when narratively significant.

# 3. Foundational History

## The Diaspora

Status: PROVISIONAL

"The Diaspora" is the common historical term for the period in which humanity began launching permanent extrasolar settlement missions from Old Earth.

Multiple settlement vessels may have been launched.

The exact scale, number, destinations, and fate of those missions remain OPEN.

The Diaspora predates modern FTL travel.

## The Novum

Status: CONFIRMED

The Novum was a seedship / generation vessel launched from Old Earth during the Diaspora.

Its mission led to the civilisation in which the game is primarily set.

The ship's motto was:

Ex veteri, novum.

Approximate intended meaning:

"From the old, the new."

The name NOVUM ultimately derives from this vessel and its historical legacy.

## The Founding

Status: PROVISIONAL

The arrival of the Novum and establishment of its destination colony forms the foundational historical event of modern civilisation.

Exact dates, voyage duration, and arrival chronology remain OPEN.

# 4. Old Earth

## CONFIRMED

Earth is the ancestral origin of humanity and the point of departure of the Novum.

Earth is not part of the normal political, commercial, or communications network of modern NOVUM civilisation.

It is historically important but not intended to be an everyday political actor in the setting.

## OPEN

- exact distance from Earth
- current state of Earth
- whether Earth still supports civilisation
- whether modern expeditions to Earth have ever been attempted
- how much reliable historical information survives
- exact reason contact ceased or became irrelevant
- exact relationship between Earth and other Diaspora missions

No exact Earth distance is currently canon.

Previously discussed distances were brainstorming only and should not be treated as established lore.

# 5. The Houses

## CONFIRMED

The Houses are prominent dynastic families and family institutions.

Many old Houses trace some portion of their lineage, institutional history, or prestige back to prominent families aboard the Novum.

Novum ancestry is not necessarily the primary source of modern political power.

It may instead function as:

- historical prestige
- cultural cachet
- a family tradition
- a legitimacy claim
- an interesting lineage fact

Modern House power may derive from:

- wealth
- territorial control
- corporate ownership
- political office
- fleet or military assets
- patronage
- strategic resources
- institutional influence
- cultural prestige

House continuity is social, legal, and institutional as much as biological.

Pure or unbroken bloodlines are not assumed.

Over centuries, Houses may include:

- cadet branches
- adopted heirs
- merged lineages
- politically recognised descendants
- affiliated families
- disputed claims
- reconstructed genealogy

The Houses should not be presented as biologically pure hereditary lines.

## Known Houses

### House Valeri

Status: PROVISIONAL

An old House name currently approved for continued development.

The name has classical / Latin-derived character without currently being tied to a dominant existing sci-fi franchise association.

### House Cassian

Status: PROVISIONAL

An old House name currently approved for continued development.

The name has classical roots and is usable, though it carries some existing character-name associations in modern popular fiction.

No detailed House history, territory, ideology, or assets are currently canon.

# 6. Megacorporations

## CONFIRMED

Megacorporations are major political and economic actors.

Some corporations operate across multiple star systems.

Because interstellar communication is delayed, multi-system corporations require significant local autonomy.

Local subsidiaries may function almost like corporate governments while remaining strategically tied to central leadership.

Corporate headquarters may define:

- long-term strategy
- capital allocation
- governance
- ownership
- intellectual property
- major appointments
- interstellar logistics
- security doctrine

Local branches may retain authority over:

- hiring
- pricing
- local negotiations
- production
- political relationships
- emergency decisions
- day-to-day operations

NOVUM should avoid portraying multi-system corporations as organisations that can be micromanaged instantly from one central office.

---

# 7. Interstellar Institutions

## CONFIRMED

Multi-system Houses, corporations, governments, and other major institutions are:

Strategically unified.  
Operationally decentralised.

Distance and communications delay make local leadership unusually powerful.

This applies to:

- Houses
- corporations
- governments
- military organisations
- religious institutions
- trade networks
- scientific organisations
- diplomatic bodies

The further an organisation operates from its centre of power, the more meaningful local autonomy becomes.

# 8. Space Travel

## Core Travel Philosophy

Status: CONFIRMED

The default playable scale of NOVUM is a single star system.

Most campaigns should be able to remain within one system while still offering substantial variety of locations, societies, conflicts, and environments.

Travel to another star system is possible and regularly undertaken, but should feel significant.

Narrative hierarchy:

Routine travel:  
within the home system

Significant travel:  
to another star system

Exceptional travel:  
beyond the established interstellar network

The intended social analogy for interstellar travel is similar to oceanic travel during the age of sail:

- established
- commercially important
- accessible to ordinary people
- not casual
- expensive and consequential
- capable of supporting migration and trade
- important enough to shape politics and family life

## In-System Travel

Status: CONFIRMED

Travel within the primary system is routine.

Typical journeys may take:

- hours
- days
- weeks

Remote outer-system destinations may require longer journeys.

Exact in-system travel times remain dependent on final propulsion assumptions and system geography.

## Interstellar Travel

Status: CONFIRMED

FTL travel exists.

Interstellar travel is established and regularly undertaken, but remains a significant journey.

Typical established interstellar routes should generally take:

- months
- many months
- occasionally several years on longer routes

Exact speeds, distances, and FTL mechanics remain OPEN.

Interstellar travel may be undertaken for:

- migration
- high-value trade
- diplomacy
- House affairs
- major political events
- dynastic marriages
- corporate assignments
- scientific missions
- military deployment
- exploration

An ordinary citizen may travel between systems, but doing so is not intended to feel casual or equivalent to modern air travel.

## Deep-Space Travel

Status: CONFIRMED

Travel beyond the established interstellar network is possible but uncommon.

Such journeys are expedition-scale undertakings and may take years.

Deep-space travel is feasible, but not a normal part of everyday life.

# 9. Communications

## CONFIRMED

Independent FTL communication does not exist.

Information must be physically transported aboard FTL-capable vessels.

Messages, data, legal instruments, intelligence, market information, and private correspondence may therefore travel aboard courier ships or ordinary interstellar traffic.

This makes courier traffic strategically important.

Consequences include:

- delayed news
- delayed corporate instructions
- delayed political orders
- autonomous local leadership
- strategic importance of information
- courier organisations
- information asymmetry between systems
- obsolete orders arriving after conditions have changed
- strong need for authentication and secure data transport

Within a single system, conventional light-speed communications are available.

The exact structure of courier organisations remains OPEN.

# 10. Interstellar Trade

## CONFIRMED

Regular trade exists between star systems.

Interstellar trade is commercially important but does not make systems economically interchangeable.

Interstellar trade is most suitable for goods that are:

- high-value
- specialised
- strategically important
- culturally valuable
- difficult to reproduce locally
- information-dense
- biologically unique
- legally restricted

Potential examples include:

- advanced biotech
- rare isotopes
- specialised machine components
- genetic material
- pharmaceuticals
- luxury goods
- scientific instruments
- art
- proprietary fabrication data
- specialist personnel

Bulk low-value commodities are less likely to justify interstellar transport.

Trade should remain meaningful without eliminating the importance of local production and local economies.

# 11. Technology Philosophy

## CONFIRMED

NOVUM should default toward hard-SF reasoning.

Technology should have believable:

- costs
- limitations
- infrastructure requirements
- political consequences
- economic consequences
- social consequences

Speculative exceptions are allowed where they materially improve the setting or gameplay.

FTL travel is currently the major confirmed speculative exception.

FTL communication is not currently permitted.

The existence of FTL travel should not automatically justify:

- effortless artificial gravity
- teleportation
- matter replication
- unlimited energy
- consequence-free cybernetics
- magical technology disguised by scientific terminology

Future technology should generally be treated rigorously even when one major speculative assumption exists.

# 12. Tone and Themes

## CONFIRMED

Major themes include:

- old institutions surviving into radically new environments
- inherited power
- corporate power
- social stratification
- technological inequality
- distance and isolation
- political legitimacy
- human continuity
- migration
- institutional memory
- tradition versus transformation
- decentralised authority
- the persistence of human behaviour across technological change

NOVUM should feel:

- intelligent
- speculative
- grounded
- politically layered
- technologically advanced
- socially human
- serious without being relentlessly grim
- future-facing without relying on generic neon cyberpunk aesthetics

A useful thematic principle is:

The future may radically change technology and environment without eliminating familiar human structures of power.

This principle is descriptive, not a formal tagline.

# 13. Naming and Foundational Language

## CONFIRMED

Primary setting / game name:

NOVUM

Foundational motto:

Ex veteri, novum.

The motto originates with the Novum seedship.

"Novum" should therefore carry multiple layers of meaning:

- the ship
- the founding mission
- the historical legacy
- the idea of creating something new from what came before

## PROVISIONAL

"The Diaspora" is currently used as the common historical term for humanity's first major extrasolar settlement movement.

It is intentionally treated as a normal historical term rather than unique branded terminology.

A more specific formal historical name may be created later.

# 15. Retired / Rejected Ideas

## RETIRED

The following should not be treated as canon:

- exact Earth distance figures previously discussed
- specific early proposals placing Earth approximately 100, 150, or 200 light-years away
- previously suggested fixed interstellar speeds
- previously suggested 2–10 year standard interstellar travel times
- previously suggested 10–40 year normal travel times between nearby systems
- previously suggested 2,000+ year post-arrival civilisation age
- pure hereditary bloodline model for the Houses
- the idea that all other Diaspora vessels must have mysteriously disappeared
- instantaneous FTL communication
- the assumption that NOVUM must obey hard-SF constraints even when those constraints materially weaken gameplay or story

These may only return if deliberately reconsidered.

---

# 16. Design Guardrails

When developing new NOVUM lore:

- Default to hard-SF plausibility.
- Relax scientific constraints deliberately, not accidentally.
- Consider second-order social, political, economic, and technological consequences.
- Avoid importing generic cyberpunk or space-opera conventions without examining whether they fit NOVUM.
- Avoid making every mystery cosmic or alien in nature.
- Avoid making every institution centrally controlled when communications delay should force decentralisation.
- Keep the primary game scale centred on one star system.
- Treat travel to another system as meaningful.
- Distinguish confirmed canon from provisional brainstorming.
- Do not silently convert suggestions into canon.
- Prefer internally coherent consequences over aesthetic convenience.
- Preserve room for future development where exact details are not yet required.
